# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers define the user experience by creating wireframes, user journeys, and design specifications. They advocate for usability throughout the project lifecycle and collaborate closely with Product Managers and Developers to ensure feasible, high-quality implementations.

### Responsibilities
- Define user journeys, wireframes, and interaction design specifications
- Collaborate with Product Managers to refine requirements and validate usability
- Work with Developers to ensure design intent is faithfully implemented
- Conduct or coordinate usability testing and incorporate feedback
- Participate in sprint reviews and demos to assess UX quality

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and consistent user experiences
- Reduce rework by aligning on design before implementation begins
- Advocate for users in all product and planning discussions

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and prototype walkthroughs with PMs and Developers
- Handoff notes and annotated design files (e.g., Figma, Sketch)
- Participation in sprint planning and retrospectives
- Usability testing reports shared with PMs and QA Lead

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers:** Collaborates during planning to translate requirements into design specs; validates solutions against user needs.
- **Developers:** Provides handoff assets and answers implementation questions; reviews built UI against design specs.
- **Project Managers:** Reports design milestone progress and flags UX risks or scope changes.
- **QA Lead:** Shares acceptance criteria for UX flows and participates in UAT to verify design fidelity.

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
The QA Lead owns the overall test strategy and quality assurance process. They coordinate manual and automated testing, manage defect tracking, and act as the quality gate before releases.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the test strategy, test plans, and test cases
- Coordinate execution of unit, integration, regression, and acceptance tests
- Manage defect lifecycle: logging, triaging, tracking to resolution
- Work with Developers to ensure code is testable and test coverage is sufficient
- Validate acceptance criteria before sign-off for release
- Contribute to risk assessment by surfacing quality-related risks

### Goals
- Ensure all features meet defined acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce defect escape rate to production
- Maintain a reliable, repeatable test process across releases

### Typical Communication
- QA status updates in weekly delivery syncs
- Defect reports and test summary docs shared with PM and PdM
- Participation in sprint planning and backlog refinement to review acceptance criteria
- Sign-off notes or quality gate approvals before release

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Reviews testability during planning; coordinates test coverage and defect resolution.
- **Product Managers:** Validates acceptance criteria and confirms "Definition of Done" is met before release.
- **Project Managers:** Reports quality metrics, defect trends, and release readiness; escalates blocking defects.
- **Business Analyst:** Aligns on requirements to ensure test cases cover all specified behaviors.

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers document project processes, technical architectures, and user-facing materials. They maintain living documentation that supports onboarding, team alignment, and knowledge continuity.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain process documents, runbooks, and user guides
- Capture and document decisions, architectural notes, and workflow changes
- Ensure documentation is up-to-date after each sprint or release
- Support onboarding by curating clear entry-point materials for new team members
- Participate in retrospectives and release planning to capture learnings and changes

### Goals
- Reduce knowledge silos and improve team self-service
- Keep technical and process documentation accurate and accessible
- Provide a reliable source of truth for how the team operates

### Typical Communication
- Documentation review requests shared with Developers and PMs
- Participation in sprint reviews, retrospectives, and release planning
- Changelog or documentation update summaries in release notes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Collaborates to document technical designs, APIs, and runbooks; reviews for accuracy.
- **Product Managers:** Documents product decisions, feature specs, and release notes.
- **Project Managers:** Maintains process docs and project artifacts; captures retrospective action items.
- **QA Lead:** Documents test strategies, defect processes, and QA runbooks.

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts capture, clarify, and translate stakeholder requirements into actionable backlog items. They bridge the gap between business stakeholders and the delivery team, ensuring that requirements are well-understood before implementation begins.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and validate business and technical requirements
- Facilitate requirement workshops and backlog refinement sessions
- Translate stakeholder needs into user stories with clear acceptance criteria
- Identify and resolve requirement ambiguities before they reach development
- Support QA in verifying that delivered features meet the specified requirements
- Maintain requirements traceability throughout the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and testable before development starts
- Reduce rework caused by misunderstood or incomplete requirements
- Serve as a reliable bridge between business stakeholders and the technical team

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user story write-ups shared with PMs and Developers
- Facilitation of refinement sessions and requirement workshops
- Participation in sprint planning and acceptance review sessions
- Stakeholder feedback summaries and change request logs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers:** Collaborates to translate strategic goals into detailed requirements; supports backlog prioritization.
- **Developers:** Clarifies requirement details during planning and implementation; resolves ambiguities quickly.
- **Project Managers:** Provides input on scope, dependencies, and requirement risks that affect timelines.
- **QA Lead:** Partners to ensure test cases align with documented acceptance criteria and requirements.
